Life at Akshara

The hours after the bell still belong to childhood.

Friends, fields, rehearsals, and the small rituals that make a school feel like a place one is sorry to leave.

An ordinary Tuesday

Assembly, work, lunch, play—and someone who knows your name.

The day is paced so that children can think, move, and rest. Morning assembly sets a tone. Classes ask for attention. Afternoons make room for the rest of a person.

Sport

Teams for those who compete, and a place on the field for everyone else. Fitness is a habit, not a slogan.

Arts & culture

Music, dance, drama, and the studio. Performances that families actually look forward to.

Clubs

Debate, robotics, gardens, and service. Children choose, commit, and discover a second home in school.

Service

Work beyond the gate. We teach that a good education includes the people it can help.

Student life

Work and play, kept in proportion.

We teach time, not merely timetable. Children learn to finish work, keep a promise to a team, and still have an hour left for a book or a friend. That balance is the culture, not a poster.

Campus news · 15 August 2026

Independence Day celebrations 2026

Sri Akshara marked the 80th Independence Day with assembly, patriotic programmes, and the national flag. Students came in uniform and in costume—army dress, traditional attire, and the small flags that make a school morning feel like a country remembering itself.

Teachers and families stood with them. The stage carried the Ashoka Chakra and the colours of the tricolour. It was a day of salute, song, and belonging.

The year, marked

Days the whole school looks toward.

Annual Day

An evening of music, theatre, and the year’s work, shared with the people who made it possible.

Science Fair

Questions made visible. Children present what they wondered about, and what they found.

Sports Day

Races, cheers, and the long afternoon when the whole school is one colour.

Cultural Fest

Food, dance, and the many homes our families come from—celebrated under one roof.

When a child needs more

Support that does not wait for a crisis.

Counselling

Academic guidance, career conversations, and a quiet room when the week is heavy. Our counsellors work with families, not around them.

Learning support

Extra help without stigma. If a child needs a different path through a subject, we build one—and we tell you how it is going.

Health

A nurse on campus, health education that is age-honest, and a culture that treats sleep and food as part of scholarship.

Parents

Meetings that are conversations. Workshops when they help. An open invitation to walk the campus you already trust.

We would be glad to meet you

Come and feel the day.

Visit during school hours. Hear the noise that is work, and the quiet that is care.
