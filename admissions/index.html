Admissions

Begin gently. We will take it from there.

We look for children who are ready to learn and families who wish to walk with us. The process is clear, personal, and never a maze.

What to bring

A simple, complete file.

We welcome children from many kinds of homes. The paperwork exists so we can care for your child properly—not to test your patience.

Application

A completed form with the details we need to know your child.

School records

Recent reports or transcripts, where a previous school exists.

Identity

A birth certificate or equivalent document.

Health notes

Immunisation records and anything our nurse should know.

The path

Four steps, each with a person at the other end.

01

Enquire

Write to us or walk in. Tell us your child’s age and what you hope for. We will reply within a school day.

02

Visit & meet

Tour the campus, sit with admissions, and—where helpful—let your child spend a little time in a class.

03

Assess with care

Age-appropriate tasks and a conversation. We look for potential and fit, not a performance for its own sake.

04

Enrol

If we can serve your child well, we will say so plainly. Paperwork, fees, and a warm orientation follow.

Visit first

The campus will tell you more than this page.

Walk the lawns, look into a laboratory, hear a classroom. You will meet teachers, not a sales script. Come with the questions you would ask a neighbour who already sends a child here—we will answer them.

Book a morning tour

Tuition

Clear fees. Real help where it is needed.

Tuition covers teaching, technology, and the run of the campus. We publish figures on request so there are no surprises. Payment plans, and scholarships for families who qualify, are part of how we keep the school a community rather than a club.

Write to admissions and we will send a current schedule, explain what is included, and talk through aid without embarrassment.
